That night, after [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/People/NPCs/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena Kolyana]] goes to sleep, the party converses with [[Ismark Kolyanovich]] learning many things. They learn that [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/People/NPCs/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd Von Zarovich]] has already bitten Ireena twice, and that he fears he will wait no longer to turn her as he believes her to be carrying the soul of his long lost love Tatyana. 

Ismark requests that they take Ireena and flee to Vallaki, a place far West where Strahd rarely visits. All of [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/Locations/Barovia/index|Barovia]] is Strahd's domain, but perhaps he will grow tired of her being so far and seek a new plaything. Hope is all they have left in this cursed land.

[[Cucur]] asks about leaving, as that's all he is truly concerned with. Ismark tells them that only the Vistani can travel through the mists, yet they all work for Strahd to some degree. There is one gathering of Vistani, however, that are not loyal to him. They currently are in a camp by the Tser Pool, just West of [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/Locations/Barovia/Barovia Village/index|Barovia Village]]. There, Madam Eva can both guide them to Vallaki and, with hope, tell them how to defeat Strahd.

Ismark also tells them that the pies they were given, by [[Morgantha]], were bought at a steep price, as they were very addictive, even to the point where Granny would take children from those lacking payment.

They ask Ismark to accompany them but he refuses, as his father [[Kolyan Indirovich]], the previous Burgomaster of Barovia has died recently and he must take up the mantle.

> "There isn't much I can do to defend against Strahd's hoard, but provide hope. So that's what I'll do until I lay fallen like my father."

Before agreeing to anything, [[Belladonna Kolyana]] speaks up, confessing to the party that she is a Dhampir, half-elven, half-vampire by blood. She is not under the control of Strahd, and that her mother, when pregnant with her, escaped Barovia. She was born outside of the mists, but is back now to save Ireena and to find a cure. After mentioning the name of Ireena and Belladonna's shared mother, Ismark confesses that she was adopted by his father but knows it not. 

Ismark also recognizes [[Lancelot]], informing the party that he belongs to Gertrude, the daughter of Mary who went missing a week ago. He offers to take care of the dog for now, as Mary is in no state to take care of anything.

Before leaving in the morning, they help carry the deceased Burgomaster to the [[Church of Barovia]] to lay him to rest in the Cemetery behind it. There Ismark knocks and the party meets [[Father Donavich]], who invites them in and leads them to the back. They party hears a screaming and howling sound from beneath the floorboards but decides not to say anything at first. They reach the prepared gravesite and Ismark begins burying his father without saying anything. Belladonna encourages him to say a few words and he does so, offering his hope that he can be a man like his father was. He was always called "The Lesser", but hopes to lose that title now that he has been thrust into his father's shoes.

After burying, they learn from Father Donavich that each night, at midnight, a host of the spirits of deceased adventurers, previous playthings of Strahd, make their way from the Cemetery to Castle Ravenloft. They also learn that the screaming was coming from [[Doru]], the Father's son. Doru had joined a company of those seeking to destroy Strahd. Instead, he returned a vampire spawn, and was now chained in the basement of the church, unable to feed. Father Donavich insisted that he would pray to Father Morning for a cure.

Before leaving, Donavich quietly asks the party, out of earshot of Ismark, to take Ireena to Krezk, as it is further away from Strahd, and more fortified.

The party hurries back to The [[Burgomaster's Mansion]] where, before they leave, are interrupted by a knock on the door from some of the Barovian Vistani. They hurry out the back.

Eventually they come to a crossroads and are attacked by 3 Dire Wolves and a Berserker. Cucur is downed but saves himself and the party defeats them. 

They come to Tser Pool and the Vistani camp. They meet Radu and he tells them the tell of a powerful mage who came to Barovia and fought Strahd, eventually falling to him though his body was never found and he may wander the land still. 

They then go into Madam Eva's tent where she seems to know things about the players. She reads their fortunes in the cards and informs them the following:

1. Vallaki has Strahd's Tome, written by himself, potentially explaining his weaknesses.
2. The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind is in a dark den, a cave of sorts.
3. A powerful sword with the means to defeat Strahd is already in their grasp, but must be awakened at the Amber Temple.
4. Strahd has an enemy in the powerful Vampire Hunter, Ezmerelda d'Avenir.
5. The final battle will take place at Castle Ravenloft.

That night [[Zelraen Nightbreeze]] madidates and uses the dead to empower his perception.

The party find tents to sleep in and collect loot, with [[Belladonna Kolyana]] finding a Wand of Secrets.
