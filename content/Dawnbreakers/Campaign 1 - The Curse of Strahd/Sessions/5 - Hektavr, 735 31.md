That night, [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/People/NPCs/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena Kolyana]] and [[Belladonna Kolyana]] discuss their shared mother, Ireena telling her sister that she was found by [[Kolyan Indirovich]] in the woods as a young girl and doesn't remember anything or why she was there alone.

Belladonna asks if she wouldn't mind if she used [[Sanguine]], their mother's rapier. Ireena agrees.

[[Andres Gaal]] wakes in the night, being drawn to [[Madam Eva]]'s tent. He finds her and she asks him to see his sword, letting him know that it was forged in [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/Locations/Barovia/index|Barovia]]. It contains the power of the Sun, one of [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/People/NPCs/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd Von Zarovich]]'s greatest fears. He will need to seek out [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/Locations/Barovia/The Amber Temple|The Amber Temple]] to awaken it.

That morning they find that [[Radu]] has prepared 5 horses for them. [[Zelraen Nightbreeze]] pulls [[Luther]] up and they share a horse, and then leave [[Tser Pools]], traveling towards [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/Locations/Barovia/Vallaki/index|Vallaki]].

They cross a massive moss-covered bridge with Gargoyles, which they destroy, just-in-case. After about a half-day's travel, suddenly they hear flapping and are overtaken by a massive swarm of bats, forming the shape of an all-too-familiar face.

> "Bring me back Tatyana!", 

shouts the face of [[Dawnbreakers/Campaign 1 - The Curse of Strahd/People/NPCs/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd Von Zarovich]] before the mass of wings overtakes the party and begins attacking, biting and gnawing at them and their horses.

The party fight the [[Swarm of Bats|Bat Swarms]], having trouble with the sheer number of them. Zelraen leaps off his horse and hides in the trees. Luther runs ahead of the group on their horse.
