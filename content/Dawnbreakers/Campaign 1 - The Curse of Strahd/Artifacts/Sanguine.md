## Description
---
A family heirloom, passed down from [[Belladonna Kolyana]]'s mother to her. A blood red rapier, used in ritual during feedings.

## Stats and Abilities
---
When used to finish off an enemy, wielder can roll 1d6 and regain that many hit points.
