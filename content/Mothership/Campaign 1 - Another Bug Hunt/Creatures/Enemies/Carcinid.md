Arthropod alien lifeforms, Carcinids were found on [[content/Mothership/Campaign 1 - Another Bug Hunt/Locations/Samsa VI/index|Samsa VI]] by the [[Omni Company]]. [[Dr. Edem]] and [[Hinton]]'s research have determined that they reproduce by emitting a shrill Shriek, as notated in Edem's [[Logbook]].

**Defeated:** 3

# Timeline
---
* ***Nov 14 2417:*** 
	* The original crew on [[content/Mothership/Campaign 1 - Another Bug Hunt/Locations/Samsa VI/index|Samsa VI]] discover a species of arthropods, and call them Carcinids, proceeding to terminate them on sight.
* [[1 - October 28th 2418]] 
	* The backup crew consisting of [[Kael Starhopper]], [[Ephraim Commander]], and [[Clanker (Unit 84-SP86)]] find the remains of a Carcinid arm in the [[Medbay]], as well as larvae in specimen containment tubes.
* [[2 - October 29th 2418]]
	* They fight and defeat a Carcinid as it erupted from a marine in the [[Garage]].
