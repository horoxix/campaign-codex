>"About damn time," comes a woman's voice from the radio.

In the Hangar, they are greeted by guns pointed at them and [[Sgt Valdez]] asking who they were. After informing them of their mission, Valdez asks for their help. She had sent several of her marines to try and prevent the [[Reactor]] from flooding, as the flickering lights foreshadowed the power turning off entirely.

At that moment, [[Dr. Edem]] approaches, shadowed by her assistant [[Ishmael Ardon]]. She asks them for immediate assistance recovering her research. The Crew says that they don't need her research, they just need her and she threatens to kill herself, saying if she doesn't have her research, she isn't coming with them.

[[HM3 Brookman]] is also there with a few of his lackeys, saying that they should just restore power to the Tower, retake it from the "carcs" and call in a Dropship.

After much deliberation, the Crew decides to help Sgt Valdez with the reactor, first because the flooding was getting worse and the power was going to shut down, second because they felt like they could use some extra marine help to recover Dr. Edem's research.

[[Ishmael Ardon]], Valdez, [[PFC Pedro]], and [[Cpl Novikov]] accompany the crew to the top of the station where they approach the edge of the "Chimney", directly above the reactor. Using enough rope for 4 at a time, they scale down, noticing a tunnel off to the side that had water pouring out of it. They ignore it and descend to the bottom. [[Kael Starhopper]] bumps into something and using his headlamp, realizes that the chimney is lined with Carcinid Cocoons. They carefully reach the bottom, ignoring the growing number of cocoons.

The enter the reactor room and find [[LCpl Franco]] on the other side of a flooded room, the reactor controls shattered beyond repair. [[PFC Weaver]] was hugging him tightly, panicking, body covered in paper cuts. Franco calls out and tells them that it was [[Hinton]] who killed [[PFC Glockner]] and destroyed the reactor, escaping into the Tunnels with [[Dr. Jensen]].

The Crew notices that there are dark shapes moving in the water, and the level is rising. A massive [[Carcinid]] erupts and pulls [[PFC Weaver]] down into the water. [[LCpl Franco]] panics and dives in, swimming for the door where the Crew stands, only to be pulled under. One of the Carcinids comes out and attacks, cracking into [[Kael Starhopper]]'s suit. They bolt for the door, shooting bullets back at the alien and knocking it into the water, as they slam the airlock open, then closed. They immediately rush Kael to the top of the Chimney, as the radiation was quickly wearing away at his body.

Kael fortunately escapes, and uses a Stimpack to heal. The Crew regroups atop the Chimney, while [[Ishmael Ardon]]'s scientific mind wants to explore the tunnel they had passed on their way down. [[Anders]] goes with him. They enter and find the corpse of [[PFC Glockner]], clutching an [[LAT-90 Rocket Launcher]], which Anders takes.

They continue on and find a rusty, red door which led to a stairway. They go down, entering a completely submerged area and find themselves outside of a jammed door. Unable to open it, they rise to the top, finding some sort of control room. Before they can investigate, however, a Carcinid falls from the catwalk and attacks. They book it for the exit. Anders takes most of the heat and damage from the Carcinid, escaping out of the tunnel, leaping onto the rope and grabbing it. He escapes out of the Chimney as radiation seeps into his [[Hazard Suit]], barely surviving with a single hit point.

Ishmael isn't as lucky. He gets hit and knocked into the water, falling out and over thirty feet to the floor below. The baby Carcinids begin to hatch from their cocoons. Radiation begins attacking his immune system, as [[Kael Starhopper]], [[Ephraim Commander]], and [[Cpl Novikov]] slide down to help, [[Clanker (Unit 84-SP86)]] goes down Ander's rope, to help him up and out of the radiation zone.

The Carcinid lands before Ishmael and screeches, knocking [[Ephraim Commander]] into a catatonic state. The others fight and defeat the Carcinid, but Kael barely scratches by and ends up with a sprained ankle.

They Crew escapes. [[Sgt Valdez]] is distraught and second guessing herself as not only did they fail to restart the reactor, but they didn't save any of her marines. As they return to the Hangar, the power goes out.

[[Dr. Edem]] approaches and asks them to help her retrieve her research. They inform her that Hinton was behind the reactor disaster and [[Sobol]] claims that he knew all along that Hinton was suspicious.

They head down the elevator shaft by sliding down into the top of the elevator, and climbing up onto the lower floor, as the elevator was stuck between floors.

Below they find the lab, quiet and abandoned, work left unfinished, [[Ishmael Ardon]] finding some research on [[Doxorubicin Research Notes]]. There is a heavy industrial decontamination airlock to the Clean Room, where Dr. Edem wants to go. Unfortunately, her codes are not working, and she believes [[Hinton]] must have changed them.

They are able to break their way in and find [[Dr. Ziegler]], working on some human-Carcinid hybrids. There is one test tube broken, with the fluid trailing to an open ventilation shaft. They try speaking to Dr. Ziegler but he only responds with:

> LEAVE...ME...HERE

The Crew finds 12 vials of [[Hydrofluoric Acid]] and begin to take them, just as a [[Carcinid]] erupts from Dr. Ziegler's shoulders, attacking the crew. Dr. Edem quickly pockets her research and they fight the Carcinid, [[Cpl Novikov]] obliterating him with a smile on his face.

They question her and she lets them know that [[Omni Company]] really only cares about her research, that the DNA of the Carcinids are a sentient cancer. She talks about the idea that everything is slowly evolving into crab-like forms. Even here, millions of light years from home, nature is still trying.

They get back to the hangar with her research and suddenly, [[Anders]] and [[Ishmael Ardon]] fall to the ground in a catatonic state, while [[Ephraim Commander]] begins digging in the ground with his bare fingers.
