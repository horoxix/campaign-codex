### **APC — Armored Personnel Carrier**

#### **Overview**
- **Type:** Ground Vehicle (Military)
- **Purpose:** Transport troops and equipment under fire or in dangerous environments
- **Common Features:**
    - Reinforced armor plating
    - Six or eight heavy-duty tires (or treads)
    - Roof or turret-mounted weapon (autocannon or MG)
    - Enclosed, pressurized interior (limited vacuum resistance)
    - Capacity for 6–10 passengers + 2 crew
